# LammaHome

Home assignment for a Fullstack Engineer role at Lamma (lamma-ed.com, an ed-tech startup).
Assignment brief: `lamma-home-assignment (1).html`.

## How to work with me on this

**Disagree with me. Out loud.** I am thinking through this problem, not dictating it. When you
think I'm wrong, say so directly and say *why* — including on product framing, scope, and
strategy, not just code. Do not soften a real objection into a "great idea, and also..."
Agreement you don't hold is worse than useless here: this assignment is graded on reasoning I
have to defend live, so an unchallenged bad assumption costs me the interview.

If you think I'm right, say that plainly too — but only when you mean it.
